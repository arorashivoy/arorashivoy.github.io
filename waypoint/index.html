Waypoint

A personal job-search tool by Shivoy Arora.

Waypoint runs on its owner's own computer. It collects job postings from public company career boards, ranks them against the owner's profile, and builds a CV tailored to each posting.

How it uses Google

Waypoint asks for one Google permission: drive.file. With it, Waypoint uploads each tailored CV as a PDF to the owner's Google Drive and creates a share link, so the CV can be included in a referral message.

drive.file only allows access to files Waypoint created itself. Waypoint cannot see, read or change anything else in the Drive.

Who can use it

Waypoint is a single-user tool. It is not offered as a service, and it has no accounts for other people. Its only Google user is its owner.
