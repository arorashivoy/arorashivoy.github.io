Cutting Room

Cutting Room is a private dashboard. Shivoy Arora uses it to publish his own short video clips to his own YouTube channel and Instagram account, and to read the statistics of those clips.

The app runs on a Raspberry Pi in his home. It has one user. It is not a public service, and it has no sign-up.

What it does

  • It lists the video clips in one folder on the Pi.
  • It suggests a title and a caption for each clip. The owner selects and edits the text.
  • When the owner selects Upload, it uploads the clip to his YouTube channel as a Short and to his Instagram account as a Reel.
  • It shows the views, likes, comments and watch time of his recent videos.

How it uses Google data

Cutting Room connects to one YouTube channel, the channel of its owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read the list and the statistics of the channel's videos. It uses the YouTube Analytics API to read the watch time of those videos. It does not share or sell this data. The privacy policy gives the details.
