Cutting Room

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Cutting Room is a private dashboard that Shivoy Arora runs on a Raspberry Pi in his home. He is its only user. It publishes his own video clips to his own YouTube channel and Instagram account, and it shows him the statistics of those clips. It is not a company and it does not sell anything. This policy tells you what the app accesses, what it keeps, and who sees it.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The app has no sign-up and no public access. Only the owner's own Google account and Instagram account connect to it. It collects no data about other people.

YouTube and Google user data

Cutting Room uses YouTube API Services. It requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload the owner's clips to his channel, with the title, the description and the visibility that he selects.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: to read the name of his channel, the list of its recent videos, and their views, likes and comment counts.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly: to read the average view duration and the average percentage viewed of those videos.

The app does not change or delete existing videos. It does not read comments, subscriptions, playlists or any data of other channels.

What the app keeps, and where

  • The OAuth client credentials and the refresh token of the owner's Google account, in a file on the Pi that only the owner's user account can read.
  • The access token of the owner's Instagram account, in the same file.
  • A list of the clips that the app published: the file name, the date, the title, the caption and the links to the published video.
  • Statistics from YouTube and Instagram stay in memory for a maximum of 15 minutes. The app does not write them to a file.

All of this stays on the Pi. There is no other Cutting Room server and no database in the cloud.

Who the app shares data with

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any third party. The app sends the owner's video and its text to YouTube and to Instagram, because that is its purpose.

To suggest captions, the app sends some still frames of a clip and the owner's note about the clip to Anthropic's Claude. It sends no data from Google APIs to Anthropic, and it does not use Google user data to train any model.

The use of information that Cutting Room receives from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube terms

By its use of YouTube API Services, the user of Cutting Room agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles data.

Removing access and data

The Disconnect button in the app deletes the stored token of a platform. The owner can also withdraw the app's access to his Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and its access to Instagram in the Instagram settings under "Apps and websites". To delete all stored data, the owner deletes the data folder of the app on the Pi.
